Why it’s handmade

Every bead is picked, placed and threaded by hand. No machine, no mould. A checker only lines up when each bead sits exactly right, so one bracelet is hours of steady, patient work.

That care is what the price pays for: a finished piece you can wear every day, made by one person in Kerala.

Details
  • Flat woven band, three beads wide
  • Seed beads set row by row on strong thread
  • Silver-tone lobster clasp with jump rings
  • Unisex. Send your wrist size in the DM for the right fit
Care

Keep it dry and away from perfume and sprays. Store flat, and take it off before sleeping or heavy work so the thread lasts.
